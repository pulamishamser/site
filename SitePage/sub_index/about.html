About Us

Last Updated: 2083 B.S.

Welcome! A warm welcome to shamser.info.np. We operate this portal with the objective of supporting the educational enhancement and digital transformation of community schools in Nepal.

Our Introduction

shamser.info.np is an educational and informational web portal of Nepal. This portal provides various digital services and information required by teachers, students, parents, and educational institutions in one place. We are particularly working to make community schools tech-friendly and to make teaching-learning simple, fast, and effective.

Services We Provide

  • Integrated Exam Management Information System (IEMIS) – Online result publication and gradesheets
  • Student and Teacher ID Card Generator
  • Digital Attendance System
  • Educational information, news, and updates
  • Teacher Service Commission and Lok Sewa preparation materials
  • Useful tools like Preeti–Unicode conversion, Date conversion, Number to Word conversion
  • Curriculum, Teacher Guides, and School Management materials
  • School Souvenirs and educational publications

Our Goal

Our main goal is to bring technology access to every community school in Nepal and make teaching-learning simple, fast, and effective. It is our commitment to make teachers tech-friendly and provide students with access to quality education.

Our Values

  • Quality: We prioritize quality in every service.
  • Accessibility: We provide accessible and easy services for all.
  • Innovation: We move forward by adopting new technologies and methods.
  • Reliability: We work to earn the trust of our users.
