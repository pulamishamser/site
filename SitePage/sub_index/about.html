About Us

Last Updated: 2083 B.S.

Welcome! A warm welcome to shamser.info.np. We operate this portal with the objective of supporting the educational enhancement and digital transformation of community schools in Nepal.

Our Introduction

shamser.info.np is an educational and informational web portal of Nepal. This portal provides various digital services and information required by teachers, students, parents, and educational institutions in one place. We are particularly working to make community schools tech-friendly and to make teaching-learning simple, fast, and effective.

This is a personal website, dedicated to the teaching profession, and we strive to make it as educational and technology-friendly as possible. The web link is https://shamser.info.np. It contains various site pages and we are working to link it with important official government sites so that all kinds of services can be made available.

Services We Provide

  • Integrated Exam Management Information System (IEMIS) – Online result publication and gradesheets
  • Student and Teacher ID Card Generator
  • Digital Attendance System
  • Educational information, news, and updates
  • Teacher Service Commission and Lok Sewa preparation materials
  • Useful tools like Preeti–Unicode conversion, Date conversion, Number to Word conversion
  • Curriculum, Teacher Guides, and School Management materials
  • School Souvenirs and educational publications

Software Tools for Schools

The website hosts small software tools useful for schools, such as online result publishing, online admission, online data entry and editing, as well as gradesheets, admit cards, certificates, and generator software.

Accounting & Audit Software

It also includes accounting management and internal audit software that is extremely useful for schools — easy enough for anyone with basic computer knowledge to operate.

Savings & Credit Software

Especially for small savings groups, a small savings and credit fund management software has also been prepared. Its demo is available on YouTube, and the link is provided on this website.
